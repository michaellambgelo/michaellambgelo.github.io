## We watch discussion-worthy films, together

That's the first thing you'll read at [jxnfilm.club](https://jxnfilm.club), and it's the whole idea. JXN Film Club is a group of people in and around Jackson, Mississippi who like movies best when they come with company — a living room full of reactions, a theater lobby deep-dive after a new release, a group chat that won't let a hot take die.

I want the website to be our clubhouse. In this post, I'll walk you through what's inside — not as the person who built it, but as a member who uses it every week. By the end I hope you'll want a seat on the couch.

![JXN Film Club banner in the club's noir style: ink-black background, the tagline "We watch discussion-worthy films, together" in large serif type with the words discussion-worthy in signal red, an Est. JXN MS stamp, and a red Join button](/img/2026-08-12-jfc-hero.png)

## Joining takes an email address and nothing else

There is no password to invent, forget, or reuse from your bank account. You give us your email, we send you a six-digit code, and you're in. Tell the site to remember your device and you won't even have to login very often.

That's the entire account. If you have a [Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com) profile you can link it — more on why that's fun in a minute — but it's optional, and plenty of members skip it.

## Screenings happen in living rooms and theaters

The heart of the club is the [events page](https://jxnfilm.club/events). Screenings come in two flavors:

- **House screenings**, where a member picks a film, opens their home, and posts it to the calendar. You RSVP with one click. If the couch fills up, a waitlist keeps things fair — and if someone cancels, you slide right in.
- **Theater meetups**, where we descend on a local theater together for something new and talk about it over drinks after.

A detail I'm proud of: when you RSVP to a house screening, the host's address isn't splashed on a public page. It arrives in your confirmation email, and only yours. Every confirmation also carries a one-click cancel link, so bowing out gracefully doesn't require an apology tour.

And hosting isn't a special privilege — **any member can host**. You pick the film, set the date, cap the guest list wherever your living room maxes out. Bringing a friend who isn't a member yet? Hosts can add guests directly, no account required.

## See what the club is watching this week

This is my favorite part of the site. If you link your Letterboxd handle, your diary feeds into the club's [Last Four Watched](https://jxnfilm.club/watched) page — and the page opens with a strip called **This week in the club**.

When two or three members watch the same film in the same week, it shows up once, with all their names on it — *Watched by* and then the movie twins you didn't know you had. That's not a statistic — that's a conversation waiting to happen. Click a name and you jump straight to that member's recent watches, complete with their star ratings, the little heart when they loved it, a *Rewatch* badge when a film pulled them back in, and — best of all — snippets of their actual Letterboxd reviews right under the poster.

You can filter the page to just the films people *liked*, just the ones rated four stars and up, or just this week's activity. It's the closest thing we have to wandering a friend's shelf of DVDs and asking, "so what did you think of this one?"

The [members directory](https://jxnfilm.club/members) rounds it out — faces, names, and Letterboxd handles, so the person you argued with about *Hereditary* at a screening is easy to find again.

## The little rituals

A few smaller things that make the clubhouse feel lived-in:

- If you attended a screening from before this portal existed, you tap **"I was there"** — and the home page keeps a friendly leaderboard of who's shown up the most. Bragging rights are everything.
- The home page surfaces **Hot Takes**: recent member reviews, unfiltered, in their own words.
- An opt-in only, occasional **newsletter** lands in your inbox with what's coming up and what members have been watching. Every issue has an unsubscribe link at the bottom if we're adding to the noise.
- Got opinions but hate typing? The [Speak page](https://jxnfilm.club/speak) lets you record a short voice memo right in your browser — takes that might end up on a future episode of the club podcast.

## Your info is treated like a guest in our home

This part is non-negotiable for us, so it's worth saying plainly. Thirty days after a screening, the RSVP contact details and the host's address are deleted — not archived, deleted. If you delete your account, your data actually goes with you. There are no ad trackers following you around the site. The [privacy policy](https://join.jxnfilm.club/privacy) is short, written in plain English, and describes what the site really does rather than what a lawyer wishes it did.

You shouldn't have to trade your privacy for a movie night. We exist to connect people organically. 

## Come sit with us

The club is still growing, and honestly, that's the best time to join one — you're not the new person in an old crowd, you're an early name on the leaderboard. If something on the site confuses you, there's a feedback button on every page and we read every message that comes in. Any suggestions for improving the site are welcome via the feedback button as well. 

Head to [join.jxnfilm.club](https://join.jxnfilm.club) today.
