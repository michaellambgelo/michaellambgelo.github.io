## Migrating from carrd.co was time-gated

For the last few years, the front door of michaellamb.dev was a Carrd page. Carrd is a fine product — it made standing up a landing page with links to my apps nearly effortless, and for a long time that was exactly what I needed. But I canceled my Pro plan and that naturally set a 6-month timeline for me to come up with another plot of real estate for michaellamb.dev to occupy.

The landing page I hosted on carrd.co was original built as a basic identity page. Once I started establishing a brand, the landing page began to feel increasingly incongruous. The blog has a design system. The apps I had added to the landing page have their own. The landing page was still stuck in the past and I didn't have quite the flexibility I've become accustomed to over how something looks.

![Carrd account plan page showing a Pro Standard plan with zero of ten available site slots in use](/img/2026-07-24-carrd-zero-sites.png)

That screenshot is my Carrd account today: zero of ten site slots in use. The landing page has moved out. And I'm ahead of schedule: the plan ends in August, and it really only took an afternoon to deploy a draft of a new landing page. I spent a couple days tinkering with it before swapping over -- that happened today.

## The new page

The freshened michaellamb.dev is a static page deployed to Cloudflare Pages, and it conforms to the michaellamb.dev design system — the same look applied to the blog. No page builder, no subscription, no third-party frame around my own front door. Just a page I own end to end, deployed the same way the rest of my projects are.

![The redesigned michaellamb.dev hero: a dark landing page with a subtle animated glitch effect on the site title, a photo, and links to the blog and contact](/img/2026-07-24-michaellamb-dev-hero.gif)

There's a subtle canvas UI effect applied to the hero — the kind of detail a page builder would never let me have. See it live at [michaellamb.dev](https://michaellamb.dev).
